# Demo run: what the results mean

This snapshot was generated by running the project with a fixed random seed. The underlying candidate returns are synthetic and were generated with **zero expected alpha**; these numbers are not market results.

The demo audit estimated a PBO of **88.6%** across 70 CSCV partitions. This means that, in this particular synthetic candidate set and split design, the best in-sample candidate ranked at or below the out-of-sample median in 88.6% of partitions. It is an illustration of how selection can fail, not a universal threshold or a prediction about live strategy failure.

The chart below shows the selection-bias experiment across 100 repetitions. As the pool of candidates grows, the median best in-sample Sharpe generally rises from random selection, while the selected candidate's median Sharpe on later unseen data remains much closer to zero.
